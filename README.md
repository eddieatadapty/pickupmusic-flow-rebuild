# Pickup Music Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/pickupmusic-flow-rebuild/**

Five ideas to turn more Pickup Music users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Pickup Music's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Open the paywall with their 90-day plan | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Ask for the account after the trial starts | +5–10% CR to purchase |
| 03 | Let them play one chord before the price | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Say $15.00 a month, not just $179.99 a year | +5–10% ARPU |
| 05 | Give the hard paywall a way out | +5–10% CR to purchase |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Pickup Music's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Pickup Music. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
